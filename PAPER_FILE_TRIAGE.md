## The invalidators — 3 HARD + 3 FLAGS (screen revised 2026-06-08 per Andreas)

The triage was first run with all six as hard disqualifiers. **Andreas relaxed three of them on
2026-06-08**: FoM3-fragility, NDE-architecture mismatch, and compressor↔NDE overlap are now **flags
to note and keep in mind, not disqualifiers**. Full text + signatures in

**HARD invalidators (a number is non-citable if any fires):**

1. **Mass-sheet leak** — pre-`--zero-mean-maps` runs (pre 2026-04-22); inflates CNN FoM3 ~25–30×; killed ~81% of runs.

6. **Cross-map leakage (full-sphere)** — cross channels are full-sphere αₗₘ-products sliced into patches ⇒ auto+cross constraining power partly **unphysical** (renders auto+cross **PROVISIONAL**, not WRONG); auto-only unaffected.

**FLAGS (note and carry as a caveat; do NOT disqualify):**

3. **FoM3 reporting** *(relaxed — FoM3 is fine)* — FoM3 may be cited as a primary metric. Keep the fragility caveat in mind (~5% marginal change → ~50% FoM3 swing; pooled vs MoS differ by the ~0.69 haircut) and report σ(w₀) + 2D areas alongside where available, but a FoM3-based number is **no longer downgraded** for being FoM3.

## Bottom line for the paper (what survives)

- **Pillar-1, auto-only — clean, citable.** At 10° (common MAF, 9000 obs/arm, zmm + disjoint), L1 ≈ CNN: σ(w₀) 0.246 vs 0.247 (tie, CNN a hair tighter on 2D). Fully calibrated (TARP-DRP + SBC + L-C2ST). Anchor: `definitive_comparison_10deg/` + memory `project_10deg_definitive_cnn_geq_l1`.

The old 20° "L1 wins ~2.5–3×" headline is **WRONG/SUPERSEDED** — driven by the hard #2 v1 noise-model bug + the newer 10° common-MAF run (NDE-mismatch and single-perm FoM3 are now only flags, but the hard reasons alone settle it).
- **Pillar-1, w₀ offset — resolved.** L1's 20° −0.37σ fiducial w₀ pull shrinks to −0.10σ at 10° and is no longer L1-specific ⇒ a flat-sky-distortion artifact, not an intrinsic ℓ₁ bias.

So even with FoM3 accepted, the BNT absolutes stay non-citable until a zmm-clean rerun. The *directional* findings survive as **BACKGROUND motivation**: (a) BNT inflation is summary-limited not fundamental; (b) turning **off** summary standardization is the decisive lever (~0.095 → ~0.79 FoM3 retention); (c) a multi-channel CNN extracts inter-bin cross-correlation gain far better than per-channel ℓ₁ (G_corr ~2.6–3.0, *on-disk JSON values — the MD reports' 3.6/195.6 are stale*); (d) plain CNN beats stock-BN ResNets on BNT parity. **The clean BNT-CNN no-inflation run — Pillar-2's "proof" — does NOT yet exist** (TO-BE-RUN).

## Pillar-2 directional findings usable as MOTIVATION (BACKGROUND, not citable numbers)

These are the within-era, relative BNT findings the paper can lean on for Pillar-2 framing. Every one carries the explicit tag **[absolute numbers contaminated, direction only]** — the magnitudes are pre-zmm (#1), FoM3-only (#3), and (for cross-method contrasts) NDE-mismatched (#4); only the SIGN/DIRECTION is paper-usable as motivation. None enters the citable-numbers ledger.

- **BNT contour inflation is summary-limited, not fundamental** — BNT is an invertible linear transform, so inflation comes from the *summary* failing to recover inter-bin structure, not from lost information. Evidenced by the strong anisotropy (σ8 std-ratio ~8.46× while all other params ~1× in `bnt_tomo4_study`) and by a richer compressor recovering near-parity. **[absolute numbers contaminated, direction only]** Sources: `bnt_tomo4_study`, `bnt_comparison_tomo4`, `cnn_bnt_losslessness_campaign`, `BNT_NO_BNT_CONTOUR_INFLATION_NOTE.md`.
- **Turning OFF summary standardization is the decisive lever** — moving from standardized summaries to `--no-standardize-summary` collapses CNN BNT inflation from the default-arch baseline (~1.80 std_sum / FoM3-ratio ~0.095) toward near-parity (~1.04 / ~0.79–0.85). This is the named "0.095→0.794" standardization lever. **[absolute numbers contaminated, direction only]** Sources: `cnn_bnt_losslessness_campaign` (+ indep/long120k/multipatch variants), `cnn_bnt_noise_curriculum_campaign`, `BNT_TOMO4_*`, `SBI_PIPELINE_BEST_PRACTICES.md`, memory `project_cnn_vmim_mass_sheet_leak` (within-era BNT note).
- **A multi-channel CNN extracts inter-bin tomographic cross-correlation gain markedly better than per-channel wavelet L1 (G_corr ~2.6–3.6)** — CNN's advantage over L1 *expands* from single-bin to full tomo4 (G_corr = R_full/R_bin_avg > 1; super-additive ~3.32 for CNN vs ~additive ~0.92–1.03 for L1/L1+VMIM), the Pillar-2 mechanism (implicit cross-correlations recovered by a CNN fed auto-maps as channels). NB: the authoritative on-disk JSON gives G_corr 3.008/2.603; the MD reports' 3.634/195.6 are stale and must NOT be quoted. **[absolute numbers contaminated, direction only]** Sources: `nobnt_tomo_bins_crosscorr_study`, `optimal_nobnt_crosscorr_benchmark`, `TOMO_BIN_CROSSCORR_DETAILED_REPORT.md`, `FINAL_SCIENTIFIC_REPORT.md` (g_corr 3.85/2.92), `nobnt_final_matrix`.
- **Plain CNN beats ResNet variants on BNT/no-BNT parity** — a plain CNN reaches near-parity without tricks while stock-BN ResNets collapse or are wildly variable, and consistency/adversarial-invariance training does not rescue ResNet to robust 5-seed confirmation. Consistent with `project_resnet_bn_contamination` (stock-BN ResNets collapse; GroupNorm/plain preferred). Caveat: plain (dim=10/120k) and ResNet (dim=6/15k) are NOT capacity/budget-matched in `cnn_bnt_parity_campaign`, so the ranking partly conflates architecture with compressor budget. **[absolute numbers contaminated, direction only]** Sources: `cnn_bnt_parity_campaign`, `cnn_bnt_resnet_split_campaign`, `cnn_bnt_noise_curriculum_campaign`, `CLAUDE_CODE_HANDOFF.md`.
- **(Baryon robustness, relative ordering)** — under baryon mismatch the CNN posterior is ~unmoved (ratio ~1.01, near 1) while per-channel L1 tightens spuriously (ratio ~1.31, large scatter); a multi-channel CNN is more baryon-robust than histogram L1. The bias *magnitudes* are normalized by a mass-sheet-inflated covariance and are non-citable; only the ordering survives. **[absolute numbers contaminated, direction only]** Sources: `baryon_bias_tomo4_study`, `baryonified_appendix`.
